### SHARING YOUR IDEA

Head to **My Ideas** to start a submission. There's no limit on how many Ideas you can have in review at once, so submit as many as you like.

You'll first be asked to review the current [Guidelines](https://create.highrise.game/learn/designer-resources/ideas/guidelines) and [FAQ](https://create.highrise.game/learn/designer-resources/ideas/general-faq). It's worth doing properly. Review is strict, and most rejections come from something covered there.

**Setting up your Idea**

1. **Upload a single photo clearly showing your item.** It needs to be one wearable avatar item, and it needs to be your own original design. This picture is what players preorder from and what our artists build to, so it should look close to the finished item. Rough or unclear submissions get rejected.

2. **Choose an Item Type.** Review all the categories and pick the most accurate one, or your Idea may be blocked. Item type also determines your item's price, so this isn't a cosmetic choice.

3. **Add a description.** Tell us about your item.

4. **Add tags.** These help players find your Idea, so be specific and thorough.

5. **Confirm your work is your own.** You'll be asked to confirm that your design isn't AI-generated and isn't based on another player's item. Breaking that confirmation means a permanent ban from Highrise Ideas.

Then hit Submit.

**About pricing**

You don't choose a rarity. All Ideas items are Legendary, and price is set by item type: most items are 6,000 to preorder and 8,000 after funding, while hair is 9,000 and 12,000. The reasoning is explained in the [Overview](https://create.highrise.game/learn/designer-resources/ideas/overview).

**What happens next**

Your Idea enters the Review Phase, where our team assesses it for content, technical feasibility, originality, and style. You can track its status under **My Ideas**.

If it's approved, it goes straight to public funding and can no longer be changed. If it's rejected, you'll get a notification pointing you back to the Guidelines.
